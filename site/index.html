Weekly Review

Turn a week of captured material into a review—not an automatic reorganisation.

RoamPrompt helps you notice themes in recent quotations, revisit open work, and draft a short synthesis. The command explains exactly what will leave your browser before anything is sent.

01 · CAPTURE

Use ordinary Roam markers

Add [[Quotes]] to eligible quote blocks and use Roam’s open [[TODO]] task syntax. No dedicated destination pages are required in advance.

02 · BOUND

Review a limited window

RoamPrompt selects the newest 50 eligible Quote strings and newest 50 open TODO strings created during the previous seven days. [[Wisdom & Quotes]] optionally marks a quote as already curated.

03 · UNDERSTAND

Know why Gemini reads them

After explicit confirmation, those selected strings go directly to Gemini so it can group quote themes, reproduce tasks for review, and draft the weekly synthesis. That semantic work is not performed locally.

04 · DECIDE

Keep every move under your control

The report replaces the bullet focused when the command starts. Original Quote and TODO blocks stay unchanged: RoamPrompt does not move, delete, complete, or refile them.

Linked references are not migration. Page links in the generated report may appear as linked references, but the source blocks remain in their original locations.

01 · ACADEMIC PAPER

Read the PDF already embedded in a Daily Note.

Run RoamPrompt: Read PDF on Current Page. RoamPrompt finds the synced attachment, reads it with Gemini after consent, and presents candidate findings with page and section locators for review.

  • Works after an Android upload synchronises to the graph
  • Separates empirical findings from interpretation
  • Keeps insertion under human control
02 · SCANNED NEWS CLIPPING

Isolate one article from a noisy historical clipping.

Attach an image-only clipping PDF and identify the target headline when useful. RoamPrompt separates the article from advertisements and neighbouring columns, then distinguishes reported events, attributed statements, and contemporary predictions.

  • Reads image-only and screenshot PDFs visually
  • Records the selected article region
  • Prevents predictions from being presented as events

Four essays on how notes become a knowledge system.

This research-grounded series begins with the centuries-old problem of external memory, moves through Niklas Luhmann and atomic notes, and arrives at a practical boundary for AI: accelerate the work while keeping evidence, interpretation, and human judgement visible.

Before the search bar

03 · ATOMIC NOTES

Why highlights never become ideas

An atomic note is not merely small. It makes one source-aware proposition clear enough to test, cite, revise, and reuse. Too broad, and links become ambiguous; too fragmented, and the knowledge network loses coherence.

04 · HUMAN JUDGEMENT

The AI question that matters

A graph can help ideas meet, but more links do not automatically create intelligence or wisdom. RoamPrompt’s Read → Structure → Review → Connect workflow accelerates reading while leaving the consequential decisions—meaning, evidence, connection, and permanence—with the user.

BYOK today · broader choice ahead

Use your Gemini key now. More LLM options are on the roadmap.

RoamPrompt does not supply a shared key. Create your own Gemini API key with Google, then enter it only in the locally loaded RoamPrompt settings until the Roam Depot listing is approved. Puli Consulting does not receive that key through this website. Each AI workflow describes the selected content and asks for consent before sending it directly to Gemini; Weekly Review also states its seven-day window and item caps.

Planned development will extend bring-your-own-key support to additional LLM APIs, giving users more choice over model capability, availability, account, quota, and cost. These additional providers are roadmap items and are not part of the current public release.

Google’s official API-key guidance ↗
  1. 1
    Open Google AI Studio

    Sign in, accept Google’s terms, and open API Keys.

  2. 2
    Create your key

    Create a key in an appropriate Google Cloud project and copy it securely.

  3. 3
    Apply it in Roam

    Open the settings for the locally loaded RoamPrompt extension and paste it into the Gemini API Key setting. After Depot approval, use Roam Research → Settings → Roam Depot → RoamPrompt.

  4. 4
    Protect and monitor it

    Never put it in a Roam block, screenshot, or GitHub repository. Review quota and billing; rotate it immediately if exposed.
